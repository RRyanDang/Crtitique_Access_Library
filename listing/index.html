The Access Now Listing

What the app says about this branch, and what the building says back.

What Access Now is

Access Now is a crowd-sourced app that rates places by their accessibility features. The listing for this branch states a few, such as accessible washroom, elevator, and free entrance. There are only two comments at the time of this report, from six years and one year ago.

What checked out

All five entrances have automatic or push-button doors at a reachable height. Pathways, aisles and furniture spacing are wide, the interior elevator buttons have braille, and all water fountains have two heights.

What did not

Two problems stood out. The washrooms are too small to turn a mobility device easily, even though the listing tags a private accessible washroom. The elevator also sounds a tone at every floor, which sits uneasily with the “Quiet” tag.

Partially Accessible. Because some parts are barrier-free and others are not, this is the rating the branch earns under Access Now’s definitions. A thin, years-old set of comments is itself an informational barrier: a patron planning a visit cannot tell which of these tags still holds.
